# Notices

## Word lists

`data/words-sv.js` and `data/words-en.js` are derived from
[FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by Hermit Dave,
built from the OpenSubtitles corpus. The FrequencyWords content is licensed
under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes made: lowercased, filtered to words made only of each language's
letters, names, fragments and slurs removed using the block lists in `tools/`, and
cut to the 5,000 most frequent entries. The derived lists are licensed under
CC BY-SA 4.0 as well.
